# Letting people try the portal: Demo Hotel, testers and feedback (2.25)

The **Demo Hotel** is a site with made-up data: about 40 doors on three floors and back-of-house areas, 24 staff with
keycards, two weeks of door events, low and critical lock batteries, a lock after new batteries with a firmware
alarm, an offline lock, a door left open, keycard requests and a keycard register. Testers belong to the Demo Hotel
only: they never see a real site, and nothing is sent to Visionline (the portal says so when they try).

## 1. Create the Demo Hotel (once)

Sign in as a platform administrator (the first administrator of the installation) → **Administration → Demo Hotel
and testers** → **Create the Demo Hotel**. It appears in your site list (the site switcher at the top left).

## 2. Invite a tester

Same page → **Invite a tester**: name, e-mail (their sign-in name; not an address that already has access to a real
site), and **1, 7 or 14 days**. Press **Make the invitation link** and send the link (WhatsApp, e-mail…), or tick
*Also send the link by e-mail* when e-mail is set up.

- The link works **once**, within **72 hours**: the tester chooses a password and is in.
- Their access then lasts the chosen number of days, **counted from that moment**; afterwards they cannot sign in
  ("Your test access has ended").
- For testers outside the hotel, switch on outside access first (EXTERNAL-ACCESS.pdf). The link then uses your
  domain, and the page shows the address where testers sign in later (`https://portal.<domain>/k-…/?hotel=demo-hotel`).

## 3. What testers see

The Demo Hotel's own colours, a band at the top ("Demo Hotel: made-up doors, people and keycards… Your test access
ends …"), and every page a hotel team uses: Overview, event log, keycards per room, Visionline cards & people, staff
keycards (requests, register, verify & extend), live occupancy, battery status, online locks & gateways, lock
troubleshooting, maintenance, monitoring, insights and charts. They cannot change settings or users.

## 4. Feedback

Everyone (testers and real staff) has a **Feedback** button at the bottom right of every page: a few words and,
if they want, a 1–5 rating. The page they were on is recorded with it.

Read it all on **Demo Hotel and testers → Feedback** (with the person, the site and the page), or **Export (CSV)**.

## 5. Between test rounds

- **Reset the sample data** puts the made-up data back as it was (fresh dates). Testers, their accounts and their
  feedback stay.
- **End access** stops a tester at once (they are signed out).
- To invite the same person again, invite the same e-mail: a new link and a new period.
